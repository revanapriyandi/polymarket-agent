# Candidate signal assessment

`POST /api/evaluation/replay` is a bounded retrospective assessment, not order-book execution replay. Owner authentication and password step-up are required; at most three assessments per minute. Input selects one exact historical profile, minimum edge 0.01–1, 7–365 days, and at most 2,000 events. `GET /api/evaluation/profiles` lists available resolved profiles for the UI; its raw count can exceed usable prospective samples.

The assessment uses the first non-abstaining stored forecast per event with a binary resolved outcome, valid recorded midpoint, creation before market cutoff and resolution detection, and unchanged rules. Model/profile histories are never pooled. It reports Brier scores against the recorded market baseline and ten probability calibration buckets. Event count and selected-signal count distinguish sample availability from threshold acceptance.

A candidate threshold selects YES when forecast probability exceeds midpoint by the minimum edge, or NO when its opposite edge meets the threshold. Hypothetical gross return uses stake 1 per selected event and the recorded midpoint. This calculation has no historical executable depth, fees, spread, slippage, partial fills, latency, allocation, portfolio drawdown, or overlapping capital constraints. Recorded order-book replay is a separate endpoint described below. No simulated fills or paper/live P&L are created by candidate assessment.

The result, input, profile and source forecast IDs are saved to the existing checkpoints table under a unique candidate ID and `candidate-assessment` stage. At least 50 usable events and 50 selected signals are needed for `assessed`; otherwise status is `insufficient`. Neither status promotes a candidate. This sample is retrospectively reused to select threshold, so a prospective holdout period and the existing exact paper profile evaluation gates remain necessary before live eligibility. Active settings, profiles, assignments and orders are not modified. No AI call or paid search is performed.

Manual verification: with no resolved forecasts the UI shows its empty state. Select an available profile and edge; authenticate and verify counts, Brier/buckets and persisted candidate ID. Raise edge and verify selected count never exceeds usable event count. Confirm results retain explicit hypothetical wording, active policy is unchanged, and a dataset below the sample threshold returns insufficient. Historical cutoff/rules mismatches and duplicate event forecasts must be excluded.

## Captured order-book replay

Migration `0003_friendly_magus.sql` adds `book_snapshots`. Each valid scanner observation stores market rules, fees, both books and observation time. Each side retains its best 100 bid and ask levels and marks truncation. Data is retained for 30 days, with deletion bounded to 5,000 old rows per hourly pass. Historical depth is collected prospectively; older periods cannot be reconstructed from price history.

`GET /api/evaluation/books` reports available snapshot count, markets and time range. `POST /api/evaluation/books/replay` requires owner step-up and accepts `{lookbackHours, maxShares, latencyMs, slippageBps, mergeCost, limit}`. Bounds are 1–720 hours, positive shares up to 1,000,000, latency 0–120,000 ms, 0–1,000 bps slippage, nonnegative merge cost and 1–500 observations. The dashboard selects at most 200 observations and displays 15 per page.

The candidate chooses a fee-aware arbitrage quote at the decision snapshot, then matches each FOK leg independently against the first later snapshot at or after latency, no more than two minutes later. Original price limits remain binding. Changed rules/tokens, stale books and unknown fees cannot generate profit. Results distinguish no trade, paired fills, one-sided fills and unavailable/stale execution evidence. Paired net deducts actual observed-depth cost, estimated fees, a conservative slippage surcharge and configured merge cost. One-sided exposure remains explicitly unvalued and never contributes fabricated profit.

Each candidate is saved in `checkpoints` as `book-replay-candidate`. It makes no ledger, order, settings or live-eligibility change. Observations are independent scenarios, not a capital-constrained portfolio backtest; queue position, competing traders, changes between observations, market impact and actual on-chain merge success are outside this calculation. Prospective paper results and the original live gates remain required. See [verification.md](verification.md) for actual local evidence.
